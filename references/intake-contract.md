# Intake contract

## Required fields

- Brand name
- Sector or category
- Offer or service
- Target audience
- Campaign goal
- Language
- Brand colors
- Headline and body typography

If a required decision cannot be found in the user's message or supplied assets, ask one concise critical question. When a sensible reversible proposal is possible, label it `proposed` instead of presenting it as approved.

## Optional fields

- Logo file and logo placement policy
- Brand voice and slogan
- CTA and destination
- Visual references
- Product or location images
- Approved proof sources
- Prohibited words, subjects, or compositions

## Classify every supplied image

- `brand_reference`: identity evidence such as a brand board, website screenshot, or approved post.
- `visual_reference`: composition, lighting, subject, or atmosphere inspiration only.
- `logo_asset`: the exact logo file allowed in rendered posts.
- `proof_asset`: a source that supports a concrete claim.

One image can have more than one role only when the user explicitly indicates that. A visual reference must never silently become a logo, palette, or factual claim source.

## Missing-information policy

- Do not invent brand colors, fonts, customer results, guarantees, certifications, or pricing.
- If colors or fonts must be proposed, store their status as `proposed` and surface that status at handoff.
- If proof is missing, replace proof-oriented content with process, education, FAQ, or transparent CTA content.
- Keep exact filenames and source notes for any evidence used in a claim.

## Brand-isolation checklist

- Search manifests, prompts, copy, filenames, and output notes for unrelated client names.
- Do not reuse another client's logo, slogan, palette, typography, imagery, claims, or campaign copy.
- Reuse only the neutral workflow, layout rules, renderer, and QA process.
